About Rentora

A simple and convenient platform for finding and managing PGs and rental properties.

What is Rentora?

Rentora is a location-based PG and flat rental platform designed to make the property rental process easier for tenants and property owners.

Tenants can browse available properties, view property details and find suitable rental options. Property owners can add and manage their properties through the platform.

🔍

Find Properties

Browse available PGs and flats and view important information such as rent, property type, features and address.

🏠

List Your Property

Property owners can add their rental properties and provide complete details for potential tenants.

✏️

Manage Properties

Owners can update or delete their own property listings while maintaining control over their property information.

🔐

Secure Access

Rentora uses authentication and role-based access to provide appropriate features to tenants and property owners.

For Tenants

Tenants can explore available rental properties, view complete property information and find a suitable place according to their requirements.

For Property Owners

Property owners can create property listings, update their property information and delete their own listings when required.

Our Goal

The goal of Rentora is to provide a straightforward platform that connects people looking for rental properties with property owners and makes property discovery and management easier.
